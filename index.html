About

I am an Associate Professor of Finance at the John Chambers College of Business and Economics at West Virginia University. My research is in empirical corporate finance and banking, with a focus on state ownership and privatization, sovereign wealth funds, corporate governance, and tax avoidance around the world. More recent work studies private equity and fintech.

My work has appeared in the Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis, The Accounting Review, Contemporary Accounting Research, the Journal of International Business Studies, and the Journal of Corporate Finance, among others. I serve as an Associate Editor of Research in International Business and Finance and on the Editorial Review Board of Corporate Governance: An International Review.
